## Synopsis

*Jed of Lagos* is a coming-of-age story about a young Nigerian man who leaves his village to pursue his dreams in Lagos — the city of dreams, the city of opportunity, the city that never sleeps.

Follow Jed as he navigates the daily crossing from the mainland to the island, battles the challenges of corporate life, forms unbreakable friendships, faces devastating setbacks, and ultimately finds love, success, and a place to call home.

This is a story about the trials of life in Lagos — the traffic, the pressure, the competition — but also about the resilience, hope, and determination that define the Nigerian spirit.

---

## Characters

- **Jed** — The protagonist, a young man from a village in southwestern Nigeria, determined to make something of himself in Lagos.
- **Bola** — Jed's best friend, a banker from Abeokuta, known for his optimism and humor.
- **Chidi** — A reclusive software developer from Enugu, who eventually joins Jed's company.
- **Tayo** — A Lagosian businessman, entrepreneurial and always looking for the next big opportunity.
- **Adaeze** — A customs officer from Onitsha, who becomes Jed's wife and the love of his life.
- **Emeka** — Jed's boss at Apex Marketing Solutions, demanding but fair.
- **David Okonkwo** — A British-Nigerian businessman who becomes Jed's partner.

## Themes

- **Resilience** — The ability to keep going in the face of adversity.
- **Friendship** — The bonds that sustain us through difficult times.
- **Ambition** — The drive to succeed against all odds.
- **Love** — The relationships that give our lives meaning.
- **Home** — The search for belonging in a chaotic world.
