# Book Proposal: "Level Up"

## Title
**Level Up: The Complete Guide to Mastering Video Games**

## Genre
Non-fiction / Hybrid (Narrative + Practical Guide)

## Target Audience
Experienced gamers (ages 18-35) looking to improve their skills and understand game design principles.

## Word Count
40,000-60,000 words

## Hook
A blend of compelling gaming stories and actionable skill-improvement strategies that transforms average players into masters.

## Market Comparison
- *The Art of Game Design* (Jesse Schell) - focuses on design, not player skill
- *Flow* (Csikszentmihalyi) - psychology theory without practical gaming advice
- *Ready Player One* (Cline) - narrative only, no guides
